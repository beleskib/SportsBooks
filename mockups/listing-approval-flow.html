Mockups for the post-pivot flow: partners no longer hit a “pending account” wall at signup. They get into the dashboard immediately and approval moves to each individual venue/coach listing. Admin gets an email + dashboard alert per submission and reviews each one in full before it goes live.

Flow at a glance

  1. 1Sign up

    Firebase auth + role pick (player / partner). No approval gate at this step.

  2. 2Submit listing

    Partner fills out venue/coach with photos, price, equipment, location. Listing saved as pending.

  3. 3Admin reviews

    Owner gets an email + an in-app alert. Opens the queue, sees full overview, approves or rejects with a reason.

  4. 4Live or rework

    Approved → public. Rejected → private to owner with a feedback note; partner edits and resubmits.
