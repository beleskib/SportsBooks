SportsBooks+ — premium tier

A subscription on top of the free product. Aimed at the 5–10% of power users who play multiple times a week — they pay a flat monthly fee and get fee waivers, priority matchmaking, and richer stats. Surfaces a small "+ Member" badge on profile and in lobby cards.

When we revisit, here's the engineering footprint

+ tier model

New table subscriptions (id, user_id, plan, status, started_at, renews_at, cancels_at) keyed off Stripe. Add users.is_plus denormalized flag, refreshed by webhook. Service-fee logic checks is_plus before applying the 5%. Priority-matchmaking ranking adds a small boost when is_plus = true.

Plus-gated stats

Win-rate by sport, best time of day, and best partner are all derivable from existing matches + match_participants + match_results tables. Materialize them in a per-user nightly job and serve from a small read-model table — keeps the profile screen snappy and the queries simple.

Stripe wiring

Two products (Monthly / Annual) with their Prices, a webhook endpoint handling customer.subscription.{created,updated,deleted}, and a customer-portal link for plan changes. Connect uses are unaffected — Plus is a B2C subscription on the platform account, not the partner's Connect account.

Surfaces to update once Plus ships

Profile self & public ("+ Member" badge), lobby cards (badge next to avatar), match-detail host card, settings root (subscription row), cancel-window logic (1h vs 4h), matchmaking ranker (small score bump). Everything reads is_plus — no scattered Stripe checks.
