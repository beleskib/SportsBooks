The Problem

📞

Manual Booking

Players still phone venues to check availability and reserve courts.

🤷

Nobody to Play

You finally book a court — then can't find enough players to fill it.

💸

Awkward Splits

One person pays, then chases everyone else to send their share.

The Solution

A single app that handles booking, matchmaking, and payment — for any sport, any venue.

  • Browse venues and coaches by sport, location, price, and availability
  • Book hourly slots in seconds with secure Stripe payments
  • Create or join lobbies to fill your court with friends or strangers
  • Split the cost automatically — no chasing people for money
